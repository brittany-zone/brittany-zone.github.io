Editorial Policy

Paul Mercier writes about Brittanys as bird dogs and family dogs, using AI tools for drafting. Every fact on field work, exercise or care needs a source Paul has read, linked right there in the text.

Paul isn't a veterinarian, so the site leaves medical topics to vets.

Readers' corrections sent through the contact page and fresh research both lead to revisions.
